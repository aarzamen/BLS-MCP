Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

What This App Does

BLS.ai is a Basic Life Support training simulator delivered via the Model Context Protocol (MCP). It runs interactive clinical scenarios, tracks performance metrics, and generates structured debriefs for BLS education.

Data We Collect

BLS.ai processes the following data during use:

  • Student identifiers — User-provided labels for tracking progress (e.g., "Turner", "Squad-3"). These are not verified against real identities.
  • BLS scenario actions — Clinical decisions made during training (e.g., "start_compressions", "administer_naloxone") with timestamps.
  • Quality metrics — Compression fraction, time-to-first-compression, shock count, pause duration, and other BLS performance indicators.
  • Instructor notes — Free-text annotations added by instructors to completed scenarios.

What We Do NOT Collect

  • No personal information (name, email, phone, SSN, DoD ID) unless the user voluntarily enters it as a student identifier
  • No cookies or browser tracking
  • No analytics or telemetry
  • No IP address logging
  • No cross-session tracking

Data Storage

Scenario data is stored on Cloudflare's edge network using Durable Objects with SQLite. Data is retained indefinitely for longitudinal training tracking. There is no automatic deletion schedule.

Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or transfer user data to any third party. Data is not used for advertising, marketing, or AI model training.

Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their data by contacting the administrator. All scenario records associated with a student identifier can be purged on request.

Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the BLS.ai administrator.
